I am a 5th-year PhD candidate at the University of Chicago broadly studying social cognitive development. I’m also a research fellow with the Institute of Education Sciences (IES) and National Science Foundation (NSF). [My research](https://vanessa-lazaro.github.io/research/) focuses on how we acquire social biases and their influence on our behavior. Prior to pursuing my doctorate at the University of Chicago, I graduated from Cornell University with a Bachelor’s degree in Psychology and Sociology and a certificate in Inequality Studies as well as worked as a research lab manager at Arizona State University.
