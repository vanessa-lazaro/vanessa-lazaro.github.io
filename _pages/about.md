I am a 5th-year PhD candidate at the University of Chicago broadly studying social cognitive development. I’m also a research fellow with the Institute of Education Sciences (IES) and National Science Foundation (NSF). Specifically, [my research](https://vanessa-lazaro.github.io/projects/)  focuses on how children think about intersecting social categories in the context of social inequality. I answer questions pertaining to the development of children’s social beliefs, and how these beliefs influence behavior. Prior to pursuing my doctorate at the University of Chicago, I graduated from Cornell University with a Bachelor’s degree in Psychology and Sociology and certificate in Inequality Studies.
