<font size="5"> <b> By embracing complexity and tackling challenging questions, my research paints a more complete picture of social cognitive processes and their consequences. </b></font>

Currently, my projects revolve around the development of social biases and how these social biases affect later behavior. Specifically, I study how to boost girls' participation in contexts that require high intellect. Previous research has found that women are underrepresented in academic fields and professions emphasizing high intellectual talents and this gender imbalance takes root in early childhood. Why? How? What can we do to mitigate this inequality?
